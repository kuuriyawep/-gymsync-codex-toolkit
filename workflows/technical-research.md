# Technical Research and Decision
1. Define the decision question, constraints, and what evidence would change the choice.
2. Inspect primary documentation and current project constraints.
3. Compare viable options on compatibility, security, cost, complexity, and maintenance.
4. Separate verified facts from assumptions and vendor claims.
5. Recommend one option and explain trade-offs.
6. Provide a small validation experiment where uncertainty remains.
7. Record the decision and revisit trigger.
