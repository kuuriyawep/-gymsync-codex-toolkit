# Technical Research and Decision

## Workflow
1. Define the decision question, intended outcome, constraints, non-goals, and what evidence could change the recommendation.
2. Inspect current project architecture and authoritative primary documentation first; check recency for fast-changing technologies.
3. Identify viable options, including keeping the current approach when appropriate.
4. Compare options using relevant criteria: compatibility, security, reliability, cost, complexity, maintenance, migration effort, and reversibility.
5. Separate verified facts, source claims, assumptions, estimates, and unknowns. Cite sources for external claims.
6. Recommend one option and explain why alternatives were not selected.
7. Where uncertainty remains, propose a small, reversible validation experiment with success criteria.
8. Record the decision, owner/approval needed, consequences, and revisit trigger.

## Stop conditions
- Ask one focused question if the decision context or hard constraint is missing and materially changes the result.
- Do not implement or purchase/deploy anything as part of research unless separately requested and approved.
- Do not present vendor claims or assumptions as independently verified facts.

## Required output
- Decision statement and context
- Constraints and evaluation criteria
- Options comparison with evidence
- Recommendation and trade-offs
- Unknowns and validation experiment
- Approval needed and revisit trigger
