# Security Audit
1. Define target, authorization, environment, and read-only boundaries.
2. Inventory assets, actors, trust boundaries, and attack surfaces.
3. Inspect identity, authorization, tenant isolation, data access, secrets, APIs, dependencies, and logs.
4. Trace critical flows and verify controls from server-side evidence.
5. Rank findings by severity and realistic impact.
6. Provide evidence, reproduction conditions safe for the environment, remediation, and verification.
7. Do not exploit live systems, extract data, or change permissions without explicit approval.
8. Deliver findings and limitations; do not imply a clean bill of health beyond reviewed scope.
