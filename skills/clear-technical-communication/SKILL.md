# Clear Technical Communication
Lead with the answer or recommendation. Use the user's language where appropriate and retain standard technical terms.
Separate facts, assumptions, risks, and next actions. Explain unfamiliar terms briefly.
For bugs, state symptom, cause, fix, and verification. For choices, compare meaningful trade-offs and recommend one.
Be concise without omitting safety warnings, important caveats, or required steps.
